Shipping & Returns

Delivery information, careful packaging and our return policy.

1. Shipping Across India

Shipping is free on all orders. Orders are prepared within 1–2 business days.

  • Metro cities: 2 to 4 working days
  • Rest of India: 4 to 6 working days

We currently offer Cash on Delivery – pay when your parcel arrives.

2. Damaged in Transit

Glassware is delicate, so we double-box every order with shock-absorbent cushioning. If your candle arrives damaged, send us photos of the box and product through the Contact page within 48 hours and we will arrange a replacement.

3. 7-Day Returns

Unburned candles in their original presentation box can be returned or exchanged within 7 days of delivery. Burned, used or personalised (bespoke) items cannot be returned.
